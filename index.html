The Rapid City Interfaith Council creates space for people and faith communities to connect with respect, pursue shared service, and contribute to a welcoming Black Hills community.

How can a faith community participate?

Invite a Council representative to connect with your congregation or community. Participation can include attending meetings, helping with service projects, hosting dialogue, or sharing events.

Can individuals get involved?

Yes. Community members can attend public activities, volunteer for service projects, and help spread the word about opportunities for respectful connection.

How do I submit an event?

Send the event title, date, time, location, short description, registration link, and contact person to the website editor or Council communications contact.
